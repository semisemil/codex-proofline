# Context Hygiene

Final artifacts must read as standalone documents, not chat logs.

## Rules

- Separate user intent from temporary wording.
- Do not copy casual chat phrases into final artifacts.
- Do not include tool comparisons unless required.
- Do not expose internal process details.
- Convert complaints or negative constraints into positive design principles.
- Mark examples as examples.

## Final check

Before final output, confirm:

- It stands alone without chat history.
- Temporary wording and unnecessary comparisons are removed.
- Examples are not treated as fixed requirements.
- The design is explained as principles, not conversation history.
